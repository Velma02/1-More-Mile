Why we run

Courage to keep going

Every journey has a moment of uncertainty, when the finish line feels distant and continuing feels harder than stopping. This event celebrates the courage to move forward in those moments. It is not about being the fastest or the strongest. It is about choosing to continue, together.

Run with purpose

Our CSR cause

Going beyond the finish line, a major portion of the net proceeds will go towards the following two phases, strengthening the impact of this community-driven initiative.

Phase 1 · January 2027

Medical support for Apeksha Hospital

Funds will go towards purchasing and donating medicines that the Apeksha Cancer Hospital urgently needs.

Phase 2 · February 2027

Support for children at the CCC Home

In collaboration with JUSLAC and CCC Line Sri Lanka, we will make a donation to support the children at the CCC Home.

By transforming every step into an act of compassion, 1 More Mile aims to demonstrate how collective action can make a difference in the lives of those facing challenging circumstances.
